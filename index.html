About

A Photographer's Perspective

After five years in Paris, I've learned that the best photographs aren't made. They're discovered.

Every photo I take starts with a feeling: the quiet tension before a runway, a smile you didn't plan, the atmosphere of a room full of art.

I'm here to capture those real moments and give them the mood of a film.
